Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language template written for the Clipped Motions website and Clipped Motions Hub. It is not legal advice.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Clipped Motions handles information for this website and for Clipped Motions Hub, our internal tool for reviewing videos and posting approved clips to our own accounts.

How the Hub connects to accounts

The Hub connects only to Clipped Motions’ own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube accounts, using each platform’s official OAuth process. It is not used to connect other people’s accounts.

Information we use

When an account is connected, we use basic account profile information and access tokens so the Hub can post videos we have approved. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with others.

Where tokens are stored

Access tokens are stored privately on our own device. They are not sold or shared.

This website

We do not collect data from visitors to this website. We do not use cookies or analytics on these pages.

Revoking access

Connected access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of the relevant platform, such as TikTok or Meta.

Deletion requests

To ask us to delete stored connection data, email clippedmotions@gmail.com.
